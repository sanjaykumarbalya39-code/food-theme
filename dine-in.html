BEFORE YOU HEAD OUT

A few details, sorted.

Answers to help you explore and plan with confidence.

Does saving a plan book a table?

No. Your plan is stored only in this browser. It does not check availability, contact a venue, take payment, or create a reservation.

Are the dining places and prices live?

This collection uses sample listings and illustrative venue photography. Prices and dining descriptions are examples, not verified venue information.

Can I plan for a large group?

Select 7 or more in the planner and choose a celebration or family occasion. Confirm seating, menus, accessibility, and any deposit directly with your chosen venue.

Can I check dietary options or accessibility?

Live venue menus, allergen information, and accessibility details are not connected yet. Confirm any specific requirement directly with the venue before visiting.

How do I change or remove my saved plan?

Change the form and save again to replace your plan. After a plan is saved, use Clear saved plan to remove it from this browser.
