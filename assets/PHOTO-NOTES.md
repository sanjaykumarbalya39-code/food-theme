# Page image collections

Photos are downloaded from images.unsplash.com and stored locally. Photos illustrate the page theme rather than verified venues or menu items.

- Home uses the original JPG files directly in assets/.
- Restaurants uses assets/restaurants/ for venue interiors, cafes, and discovery.
- Dine In uses assets/dine-in/ for events, shared tables, and dining occasions.
- Explore Menu uses assets/explore-menu/ for dishes and ingredients.

Images may be reused within their own page. No photograph is shared between page collections. Keep new page images within the matching directory.

- About uses assets/about/ for ideas, collaboration, ingredients, and local context. Its people photos are illustrative and do not represent the company team.

- Contact uses assets/contact/ exclusively for conversation, writing, and workspace images. Workspace photos do not represent a company office.

- FAQ uses assets/faq/ exclusively for reading, reference, planning, and help imagery. It does not depict a staffed support centre.

## Dish photographs

Explore Menu has a separate local photograph for each of its 72 dishes in assets/explore-menu/dishes/. The source image URLs and titles are recorded in that directory's sources.json. They are illustrative images of the named dishes, rather than verified photographs from listed restaurants.

- Cart uses assets/cart/banner.jpg (Kite Packaging takeaway image) and assets/cart/empty.jpg (Tablerie table setting), exclusively for its page backgrounds. Cart item photos intentionally match the selected dish from Explore Menu.
