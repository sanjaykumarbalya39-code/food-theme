How do I find a restaurant?

Use the search at the top of the page to search the sample restaurants by name, cuisine, or location. You can also browse the Dine In section.

Can I book a table or place an order?

This website is currently a preview. Browse the restaurants and food inspiration; live table bookings, menu prices, and ordering are not available yet.

How do I change my delivery location?

Choose a city using the Deliver to selector in the header. Your preference is remembered on this device. Restaurant listings currently show sample data.

Which languages are available?

The header supports 44 languages, including English, Hindi, Kannada, Arabic, French, Chinese, and more. Select your preference in the header. Full page translations are coming soon.
