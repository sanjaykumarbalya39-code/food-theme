16 answers to explore

Why might a search show fewer results than expected?
Finding your way

A search is combined with the category and other filters already selected. Clear the search or use Reset filters to return to the complete sample collection.

Can I search for an ingredient as well as a dish?
Finding your way

On Explore Menu, search checks dish names, categories, and sample ingredient descriptions. Restaurant and Dine In searches instead match venue names, cuisines, and neighbourhoods.

What does Recommended mean in the sorting menu?
Finding your way

Recommended restores the original editorial order of the sample collection. It does not use personal profiling, paid placement, or a live recommendation service.

Does selecting a food category place anything in my cart?
Finding your way

No. Category buttons only change the collection being shown. Exploring a dish, opening venue details, and saving a favourite do not create an order.

Will favourites remain if I use a private browsing window?
Your browser & saved items

Private browsing may remove browser storage when the session ends. For a longer-lived shortlist, use a regular window and keep the browser storage for this site enabled.

Why are my saved items different on another phone or laptop?
Your browser & saved items

Saved dishes, saved venues, and preferences belong to the browser where you created them. There is no account-based syncing between browsers or devices in this preview.

What happens when I clear the site data in my browser?
Your browser & saved items

Browser-local favourites, city and language preferences, and saved dining plans can be removed. Clearing site data does not cancel a booking because this preview creates no real bookings.

Can I remove one favourite without clearing everything?
Your browser & saved items

Yes. Find the saved dish or restaurant and press its filled heart again. The Saved collection will update to reflect the change.

Does the location selector use my device GPS?
Preferences & planning

No. The header uses a city you select manually. It does not request geolocation permission or calculate live distances from your device.

How does the dining planner interpret a selected time?
Preferences & planning

It stores the date and time exactly as you choose them, without a time-zone conversion or a venue availability check. Confirm timing directly with your chosen business before making real plans.

Can I use the contact form without sharing my draft?
Preferences & planning

Yes. You can prepare and review the text entirely within the current page. Copy and download are optional actions you control, and neither action sends the message to a recipient.

What does a sample price leave out?
Preferences & planning

A sample price is an illustration, not a quote. The preview does not verify portion sizes, taxes, service charges, booking conditions, or final restaurant pricing.

Why do some animations look different on my device?
Access & troubleshooting

The site reduces movement when your operating system or browser requests reduced motion. Content and controls remain available even when decorative transitions are limited.

How can I explore the site without a mouse?
Access & troubleshooting

Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between links and controls. Enter activates links, buttons open dialogs, and native question panels can be opened with Enter or Space. Escape closes a dialog.

Why might a photo or font load differently?
Access & troubleshooting

Photos are bundled with the site, while the optional web font needs an internet connection. If the font cannot load, the browser uses a system fallback. A slower connection or cached files can also affect the first load.

What if the copy button cannot access my clipboard?
Access & troubleshooting

Clipboard permissions and browser security settings can block copying. Select the prepared text manually or choose Download .txt on the Contact page instead.

WHEN SOMETHING FEELS OUT OF STEP

Three small checks before you retry

Start with the simplest explanation, then work towards the setting that may need attention.

01

Check the current filters

A selected category and a search phrase work together. Reset both if the results look unexpectedly narrow.

02

Check the browser context

A private window, disabled storage, or a different device can explain why favourites or preferences are missing.

03

Keep a useful description

If the issue continues, note the page, the action you took, and what you expected. Leave out private account or payment information.
