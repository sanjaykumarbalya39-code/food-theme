BEFORE YOU PICK YOUR TABLE

A few things worth knowing

Helpful answers for exploring the restaurant collection.

Are these live restaurant listings?

These are sample listings for the website preview. Prices, ratings, and offers are illustrative; venue photographs show dining inspiration rather than verified restaurant interiors.

Can I make a reservation here?

Use Book a table to choose a future date, time, and party size, then save a booking preview on this browser. My bookings lets you review or remove it. Restaurant availability is not checked and no real reservation is sent.

How do filters and search work?

Search matches restaurant names, cuisines, and neighbourhoods. Cuisine filters and sorting work together. Reset filters to return to the complete sample collection.

Will changing my city show different restaurants?

Your chosen city is saved as a preference. For now, the directory displays the same Bengaluru-area sample collection regardless of the selected city.

Where are my saved restaurants stored?

Saved places are kept in your browser on this device. They do not require an account and are not synced across devices.
