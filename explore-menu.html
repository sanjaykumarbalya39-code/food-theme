A FEW INGREDIENTS OF CLARITY

Before you pick your favourites

Helpful details about browsing this menu preview.

Can I order these dishes?

This is a food inspiration and menu preview. You can explore details and save dishes, but live ordering, restaurant stock, checkout, and payments are not connected.

Are the photos and prices from real menus?

The photos illustrate the style of food. Dish descriptions, recipe ingredients, and prices are sample content rather than verified restaurant menu information.

What does the plant-based filter mean?

It shows recipe concepts with plant-based ingredient lists. It does not verify restaurant preparation or allergen safety. Always confirm ingredients and cross-contact with the venue.

Where can I find allergen information?

The dish detail panel shows possible allergens for the illustrative recipe, not an exhaustive or verified list. Check with the restaurant before ordering if you have an allergy or dietary requirement.

Where are my saved dishes kept?

Favourites are saved in this browser on this device. Select Saved dishes to view them, or press the heart again to remove a dish from your list.
